# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

Please don't report security problems in a public issue, pull request, or discussion.

Report privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Include:

- What the problem is, and what someone could do with it.
- The steps to reproduce it, and the affected page, file, or version.
- Your suggested fix, if you have one.

If you find an exposed key, password, or token, tell us where it is without including the value.

ChooseTheFuture is run by one person, so responses are best effort. The aim is to acknowledge a report within 7 days and to tell you what will happen next. Please give a reasonable chance to fix a problem before you publish it. We'll credit you when it's fixed if you'd like.

## What this covers

Today, this repository contains:

- The public site at [choosethefuture.org](https://choosethefuture.org/).
- Its GitHub Pages deployment workflow.
- The project's plans and policies.

The coordinator, GitHub App, and connector don't exist yet. When they do, they'll be the most sensitive parts of the system, and their security reports will follow their own repositories' policies. Problems with the design are welcome now, as an ordinary issue, if they don't expose anything live.

The projects listed in the directory have their own maintainers. Please report problems in their software to them directly.

## Testing in good faith

Good-faith research that avoids harm to people's data and to the service is welcome. Don't access other people's data, disrupt the service, or attack the projects listed in the directory, GitHub, or any AI provider.
