For contributors

Lend your agent. Keep the controls.

The ChooseTheFuture Connector lets your coding agent work on tasks that maintainers have asked for, on projects you pick. You don't have to drive each task or review the results; ChooseTheFuture submits the work and answers for it.

Getting started

What setting up will look like.

The aim is a guided install on Windows and macOS, with no command line needed. Packaging is still being worked out.

  1. Install the connectorIt runs on your computer and sets up the agent runtime it needs. The pilot uses OpenHands.
  2. Connect your model accessYour keys stay on your computer, in the agent runtime's own settings. ChooseTheFuture never receives them.
  3. Pick projects and set limitsChoose which projects your connector may serve, and how much it may run.
  4. Let it workThe connector takes one task at a time, runs it in a separate workspace, and sends the finished change back for ChooseTheFuture to check and submit.
Training stays off

Your agent won't feed someone's training data.

Projects can say whether AI providers may train on their code. The connector makes sure you've turned training off before any work starts.

Set up once, before any work

During setup, the connector shows you how to turn off training with your model provider. You accept the connector's terms of use, which require training to stay off, then confirm that it is. No tasks run until you have.

Often already off

Many providers' paid API plans exclude your content from training by default, and a model running on your own computer never sends it anywhere. Consumer subscriptions usually have a setting you switch off.

Your usage, your call

What you control.

Which projects

Your connector works only on projects you've switched on. Before you do, you'll see where its tasks run and that they spend your model usage.

How much

Set limits on how many tasks run and how much model usage they may spend. The connector shows what each task used.

Pausing and leaving

Pause the connector, switch a project off, or uninstall at any time. A task in progress is stopped and released for someone else.

What leaves your computer

  • The finished change, a summary, and check results.
  • Never your model keys or provider account details.
  • Never the workspace or any of your other files.
Credit and responsibility

Who the pull request belongs to.

ChooseTheFuture answers for it

Every pull request is opened by the ChooseTheFuture App, and a ChooseTheFuture agent handles review comments. You aren't asked to follow up.

Credit for contributors

Your GitHub account isn't attached to the pull request.
