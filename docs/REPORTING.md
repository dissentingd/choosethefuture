# Reporting process

How to report a problem with ChooseTheFuture, and what happens next. ChooseTheFuture is run by one person, so timings are best-effort goals rather than promises.

## What to report, and where

| Problem | Where |
|---|---|
| Stale, incorrect, or unwanted project information | [Report form](https://github.com/dissentingd/choosethefuture/issues/new?template=report-listing.yml), or "Report this listing" on the project's page |
| Unsafe or harmful guidance, spam, or abuse | [Report form](https://github.com/dissentingd/choosethefuture/issues/new?template=report-listing.yml) |
| An unwanted or low-quality ChooseTheFuture pull request | [Report form](https://github.com/dissentingd/choosethefuture/issues/new?template=report-listing.yml), or close the pull request |
| A possible breach of the AI training policy | [Report form](https://github.com/dissentingd/choosethefuture/issues/new?template=report-listing.yml) |
| A security problem | [`SECURITY.md`](../SECURITY.md), not a public issue |

Issues are public. Don't include private details about anyone. If you can't report something without private details, open an issue saying you need a private channel, and leave the details out.

## What happens next

1. **We look at it**, aiming to respond within 7 days.
2. **We act quickly where harm is likely.** A listing or guidance that may be unsafe, or that a maintainer doesn't want, can be hidden while we check it.
3. **We check it** against the project's own repository and maintainers.
4. **We fix it or explain why not**, in the issue, and close it.

## Maintainers come first

A maintainer who wants a listing removed or changed doesn't need to give a reason. Tell us how to confirm you're a maintainer, such as a link to your commit history or the project's maintainers file, and we'll act on it. We aim to do this within 2 days.

A maintainer can always stop agent work without contacting us: remove the label from an issue, or uninstall the ChooseTheFuture App.

## What we may do

- Correct a listing or mark it **not recently confirmed**.
- Hide or remove a listing, a goal, or guidance.
- Withdraw or close a ChooseTheFuture pull request, and lower or pause pull requests to a repository.
- Remove abusive content from this repository, and block people who repeat it.

Once the connector exists, this will also include suspending a connector that breaks the rules.

## If you disagree

Reply to the issue with what we got wrong, or open a new one. Community interest alone doesn't keep a project listed against its maintainer's wishes.
